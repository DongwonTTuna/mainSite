Privacy Policy — gws CLI

Effective 2026-10-25

This page covers the Google OAuth client named “gws CLI” (Google Cloud project gws-ttuna0790-260607). It is a personal tool. Only its owner, Dongwon Lee, uses it, to work with his own Google account from his own computers through the Google Workspace CLI. It is not offered to anyone else.

What it accesses

Data in the owner’s own Google account, such as Gmail, Google Calendar, Drive, Docs, Sheets, Slides and Tasks, only as needed for the owner’s own work.

How the data is used

The data is read or changed only for the owner’s own tasks. AI assistants that the owner uses may process it on his behalf. It is not sold, not used for advertising and not shared with anyone else.

Storage

OAuth tokens are stored encrypted on the owner’s computers. Copies of data are kept only in the owner’s own files.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time from Google Account → Third-party connections.
